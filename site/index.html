What it does

Everything a confidential wallet needs, one keypress away.

Confidential sends
Pick a contact, type 25, done. Amounts are encrypted on your machine before they leave it.

One signature, every balance
A single EIP-712 signature decrypts all your tracked tokens.
w · wrap
Wrap and unwrap
Move between public ERC-20s and their confidential twins, exactly.

History you can read
Incoming and outgoing transfers, decrypted when you ask.
: · command bar
Fast as a shell

d · doctor
Know your setup

Send

Checked before you sign.

Every send is simulated and checked against your decrypted balance (an ERC-7984 transfer above your balance silently moves 0), and a five-second countdown lets you stop it with Esc.

Wrap and unwrap

Public ERC-20 in, confidential tokens out.

Exact approvals, never unlimited. Dust that cannot be wrapped is shown before you confirm, and an interrupted unwrap resumes without sending anything twice.

Scripting

The same wallet, as commands.

Every screen has a one-shot command with the same checks. Add --json for one machine-readable document and precise error codes. 

$ fhew send bob 25 cusdc
$ fhew wrap 100 czama
$ fhew balance --json --password-stdin
$ fhew history --decrypt
$ fhew doctor
# try it with no funds: FHEW_BACKEND=mock FHEVM_WALLET_HOME=$(mktemp -d) fhew
Security

Built so a mistake never costs you twice.

Keys stay local

Keystores are encrypted with your password, and signing happens on your machine.

Never sent twice

A transaction that may still land is never retried; you get its hash instead.

Owner-only data

Wallet files are private to your user, and folders others can change are refused.

Mainnet needs intent

Scripts need both -n mainnet and --mainnet before anything is signed.
